# Made in Dilan — Content Planner

A personal workspace for turning raw ideas into stories, platform-specific production plans, shot lists, and clear next steps.

Use it for design projects, creative experiments, professional experiences, and personal ideas. Each new plan starts with its own brief, facts, available material, and intended audience.

Built for designers and independent creatives who have work to share and need a practical plan for explaining it on camera. Bring a project and the material you already have; work through what to say, what to show, and what to record first.

## Start with an idea

Download and extract the [ZIP package](https://github.com/madeindilan/made-in-dilan-content-planner/archive/refs/heads/main.zip), then open this repository in Codex and write:

> $made-in-dilan-content Help me develop my new content idea in my own voice. First understand the idea and the material I have. Then prepare the platform plan, spoken words and visuals, editing notes, and ordered tasks. Keep the first production small enough to start.

Repository documentation and examples are in English. Generated content follows the language requested in the brief; when no language is specified, use the language of the user's brief.

## Make it your own

The default [personal context](.agents/skills/made-in-dilan-content/references/dilan.md) reflects Dilan's practice and preferences. Replace it with your own background, voice, references, and production constraints before planning your content. Start a new brief with your own project facts and assets; use the worked example to understand the structure.

## Choose the level of preparation

| Mode | Best for | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Quick recording | One observation or design decision | Talking points, essential visuals, and the next task |
| Content package | Adapting an idea to selected platforms | Platform plan, a detailed production table for the first piece, and tasks |
| Project story | A longer YouTube video or case study | Story sections, supporting material, narration, and an editing and sound plan |

Durations are working estimates. Choose the platforms and formats that serve the idea.

## Sources and attribution

The method library includes short summaries of general storytelling, filming, editing and sound principles, together with original applications for Dilan. Source identities and video links are kept outside the public package; the method summaries themselves are retained. This is an independent workspace. The source register distinguishes reviewed transcript text or sections, descriptions and secondary summaries, and material awaiting review.

Full transcripts, paid course materials, and music files are not included. The [verification record](docs/verification.md) documents the checks and remaining input needed for the worked example.

## What runs here

The reusable agent skill prepares plans and drafts when invoked. The repository does not automatically create videos, publish posts, or run scheduled Pinterest scans. When research is requested, document the sources, review dates, observed techniques, and original adaptations in the research table.
