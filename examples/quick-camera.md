# Explain one decision on camera

**Purpose:** Explain a project decision in your own words. The first practice recording does not need to be published.

Open the final design in front of you. Stabilize the phone and check the audio in a quiet place. Keep only three talking points beside the camera:

1. The detail I want to show.
2. The choice I made and my actual reason.
3. Where that choice appears in the design.

Answer as if a friend asked, “Why did you do this part that way?” If you stumble, repeat that short section; a complete restart is optional. You can try audio first, then record yourself on camera.

| Pass | What to do | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Raw explanation | Explain one decision for roughly a minute | Your own words and genuine rationale |
| Listen back | Mark repetition and any missing reason | One short revision note |
| Second take | Explain it again using the note | A usable spoken draft |

The assistant can then turn the recording into a scene table. This practice uses Dilan's own explanation and the material available for her project.
