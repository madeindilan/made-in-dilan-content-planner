# 3. Make the filming decisions concrete

**Piece:** one Instagram Reel, roughly 40 seconds.  
**Status:** draft narration; shots and tasks planned.  
**Timing:** estimates to check against the first rough spoken take.

## Filming table

| Scene / estimate | Spoken words — draft | Visual to record | Editing / sound purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · 0–6 sec | “I want to make a video about Content Planner. Here’s how I plan the recording.” | Medium on-camera shot, phone stabilized. | Start with the speaker’s voice; establish the topic. |
| 2 · 6–13 sec | “My brief names the topic, the audience, and the material I already have.” | Screen-record the raw idea and material list in [01-brief.md](01-brief.md). | Cut to the file as these items are mentioned; hold the text still. |
| 3 · 13–21 sec | “The plan keeps one message: turn an idea into a clear first recording task.” | Show the main thought and three beats in [02-plan.md](02-plan.md). | Keep the key sentence readable; omit unrelated scrolling. |
| 4 · 21–32 sec | “Here, each spoken line sits beside its visual and editing note. I can see what to capture.” | Screen-record this table, pointing across one row. | Follow the columns in speech order; use readable crops of the same file. |
| 5 · 32–40 sec | “My first task is a rough spoken take. Then I record the screens that support it.” | Show task 1 below, then return to the on-camera speaker. | End on an actionable step; leave the voice clear. Music is optional. |

Use the saved files as present-day demonstrations of the workflow. Adjust the draft speech to Dilan’s own phrasing before a final take.
