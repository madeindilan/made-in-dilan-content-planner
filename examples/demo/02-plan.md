# 2. Turn the idea into a plan

**Make:** one Instagram Reel showing how a broad idea becomes a filming plan.  
**Main thought:** a useful plan connects what to say, what to show, and what to do next.  
**Intended feeling:** clarity and a manageable place to start.  
**Scope:** quick recording; approximately 40 seconds, adjusted after a rough spoken take.

## The story in three beats

1. **Start with the idea.** Show the brief: a topic, an audience, and available material.
2. **Make the recording decisions.** Show the plan and the rows connecting speech, visuals, and editing.
3. **Choose the first action.** End on the rough-recording task.

## What to capture

- A short on-camera opening and ending.
- The actual [brief](01-brief.md), this plan, and the [filming table](03-shot-table.md).
- A readable view of the first task at the bottom of the filming table.

## What the planner resolved

| Starting point | Decision in the plan |
| --- | --- |
| “Show how I use Content Planner” | Follow one idea through three saved artifacts |
| A broad product explanation | One message in one Instagram Reel |
| Files available for screen recording | Show the exact brief, plan, and filming table |
| A preference to appear on camera | Keep the speaker in the opening and ending |
| “Where do I start?” | Record one rough spoken take first |
