## A simple phone setup

- Stabilize the phone and use soft side light. Keep only a few relevant elements in the background.
- Record short spoken sections. If the physical product is unavailable, demonstrate the portfolio file; do not fabricate an unboxing scene.
- Three essential visuals: the complete package, a readable label, and a series comparison. Camera movement is optional.
- Place rough narration into the Premiere timeline first. Add missing visuals next; polish color and effects after the sequence works.
- Use an actual click or paper sound only when it supports the action. Music must leave the voice clear; effects are optional.
