# Platform adaptation

These are editorial recommendations for Dilan's goals, rather than verified platform algorithm rules. Verify current numerical limits, features, or trends against official sources when needed.

| Platform | Editorial role | A complete plan includes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reel | A visual story about one decision or moment | Visual opening, natural speech, meaningful detail, a simple ending, and caption |
| Instagram carousel | Let people inspect decisions visually | Cover, page sequence, copy and visual for each page, and caption |
| Instagram Story | Share proximity to work in progress | A short sequence using real material available that day; a meaningful question when useful |
| YouTube | Follow the reasoning and process | Main curiosity, sections, evidence, talking points, sound/editing, title, and thumbnail direction |
| YouTube Shorts | Answer one concrete question independently | Its own opening, readable detail, and complete payoff without depending on a longer video |
| LinkedIn | Show professional judgment and attract suitable opportunities | Constraint, actual role, decision and reason, observable output, and an appropriate text/document/article format |
| Pinterest publishing | Make a design example discoverable | Pin visual, descriptive title, description, suitable board, and correct destination |
| Pinterest research | Turn references into original ideas | Source link, review date, observed technique, original adaptation, and production effort |

## When current inspiration research is requested

Research does not authorize publishing. Inspect accessible examples and report the actual sample size. Recent appearance is not proof of popularity or performance. Do not invent unavailable save or reach metrics.

## Adaptation check

Reuse suitable footage and assets. Rebuild the message, title, visual order, and ending around each platform's purpose. Avoid automatically posting the same video everywhere or requiring every possible format.
