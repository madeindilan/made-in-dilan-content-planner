# Research policy

This public release contains planning instructions and worked applications for Dilan's own workflow. External teaching notes and their research history are not distributed in this package.

## When a new brief needs research

Use material the user provides or authorizes you to inspect. Record the source, review date and what was actually available. A transcript supports spoken wording; a claim about framing, transitions or sound needs the corresponding audiovisual evidence. Do not imply that a complete video was watched when only a summary was read.

Turn observations into an original application to the user's project. Keep quoted wording limited and identify its source. Do not copy a third party's script, lesson, images, music or distinctive exercise into a deliverable without the necessary permission or license.

If a source's reuse terms require attribution, preserve that attribution. If the requested output cannot include it, exclude the source-dependent material or obtain suitable permission. Removing a name does not create permission to reuse a work.

Use the [source card](../assets/source-template.md) to record research for a new project. Do not represent an empty research register as completed research.

Publishing and future scheduled scans require their own user request.
