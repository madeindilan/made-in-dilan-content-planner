# Made in Dilan content production

Turn an idea into a plan that makes Dilan's experience visible and gets filming started. Read the [personal context](references/dilan.md). More recent preferences in the conversation take priority.

Documentation and examples are English. Create new content in the language requested by the user; otherwise follow the language of the brief.

## Choose the scope

Use the requested level. **Quick recording** suits one observation or decision; **content package** suits platform adaptations; **project story** suits a longer video. If unspecified, propose the first producible piece and state the scope assumption in one sentence. Include only requested platforms.

## From idea to recording

1. Read the brief, available visuals, and earlier decisions. Ask for the most important missing fact only when it changes the story; continue independent preparation. One genuine explanation of a decision is more valuable than an assumed storyline.
2. Choose the main thought and intended feeling in one sentence each. Build a story around a real obstacle or change when supported. If only the final work exists, explain the decision from today's perspective; do not invent historical before-and-after material or emotions.
3. Use the [production decision guide](references/methods.md) for the selected scope. Follow the [research policy](references/sources.md) when a new brief needs external material; preserve required attribution or exclude source-dependent material.
4. Connect each chosen reference or project detail to a specific scene and explain its purpose. Keep observed facts separate from proposed creative direction. Transcripts support spoken content; claims about framing or editing require visual evidence.
5. Start with talking points or rough audio, then identify essential visuals. Three talking points may be enough in quick mode. A full script is optional. Preserve a preference to appear on camera; offer voiceover as an alternative.
6. Use the [platform guide](references/platforms.md). Adapt the purpose, opening, sequence, and ending while reusing suitable material. Filmmaking methods are not platform algorithm rules.

## User-facing output

Use the [production template](assets/production-template.md), retaining only relevant sections:

- One sentence: what to make and why.
- When requested, a platform table: platform, original angle/format, material, status.
- A scene table for the selected piece: section/target duration, natural words or talking points, concrete visuals, editing/sound purpose.
- A task table: one action, owner, dependency, completion criterion, status. Put the next actionable step first.
- One short question if an unknown fact changes the story. Put source and technical notes after the main plan.

Quick mode needs only a short scene table and a few tasks. Explain how a suggested visual or sound will be produced and what it contributes. Silence and a static frame are valid choices.

## Before finishing

Does the speech sound natural for Dilan? Is the reason for a decision explained? Does each spoken section have a visual? Can filming begin from this plan? Is task completion observable? Label missing reasoning **awaiting rationale** and proposed copy **draft**. Do not invent personal memories, performance results, or client outcomes.

This is a planning workspace. Publishing, account interactions, and future scans require the corresponding explicit user request. Use the [source card](assets/source-template.md) when adding material.
